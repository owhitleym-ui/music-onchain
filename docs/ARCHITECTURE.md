# Architecture

## What we are building

Fans make 3 picks per month on artists under about 5,000 listeners. Lenders fund artist advances. An AdvanceDeal contract splits incoming USDC royalties between artist, lender, scouts, and platform, and closes itself when repaid.

## Not building yet

- Perp/index market
- Tranches and reserve
- Real royalty ingestion
- zkTLS listening proofs

## Legal posture

Testnet prototype. Fans give signal, not money. Production lending would go through licensed partners.
